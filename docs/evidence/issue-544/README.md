## What the figures do—and do not—show

The [recording](walks.json) has the same fixed two-wall checks as #542 (effective win, attrition, lost ground, and Hone-funded return) plus a **constructed production** Corundum branch. Corundum has 3,600 HP against Basalt's 35 (about **103×**); max Hone hits for 512 compared with the starter's 4 (**128×**). Two otherwise identical branches start with 24 Sentinel HP, one plate, two six-charge Blades, and the same cadence. At 24 seconds rank 3 (32 damage/hit) leaves 3,597 wall HP and a fallen Sentinel; rank 4 (512/hit, final rank costs eight Arete) clears the wall with 8 Sentinel HP. Each Blade has two charge left in both branches. Era-earned, previous victories, the second Blade and preloaded reserves are constructed inputs; rank and seat purchases are real actions. This establishes a meaningful outcome difference, not the availability or cost of reaching it through representative play.

The played Shale checkpoint is one actual live-acquisition sample on the declared long-session schedule. It does not establish median first-clear time, repeatability across policies and seeds, or acquisition of late opposition and max Hone. In particular the short seed-23 pushing walk can reach its independent frontier stop without clearing Shale. Its incomplete combat route is reported honestly, not made a success by the historical `w0` record. Full live balance recalibration was not done; no frozen numerical baseline was edited.
