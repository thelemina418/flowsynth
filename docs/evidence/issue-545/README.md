## What the comparison shows

The seed-468 pushing recipe reaches a living, unplayed Shale on day 3 after earning a two-Arete bank. At that checkpoint, a new habit takes Blacksmith and practices for 600 credited seconds with the fight unplayed. Blacksmith banks one deterministic choice for that pair. It consumes no Arete, adds no module roll, and mints no gear until the player chooses. Each branch has Sentinel and Guard Plate in identical seats, and one weapon in the same adjacent seat. Unfed branches shelve all Columns. Routed branches use the already-earned Flywheel reserve and the starter Column through ordinary module and World placements. No reserve, item, HP, or currency is injected.

| Weapon and supply | Shale HP at 4 s | Shale HP at 8 s | Shale / Sentinel HP at 32 s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Charge Blade, unfed | 48 | 45.7 | 42.1 / 0 |
| Striker, unfed | 43 | 36.4 | 2.8 / 0 |
| Maul, unfed | 48 | 33.7 | **0 / 3** |
| Striker, routed | 40 | 30.4 | **0 / 9** |
| Maul, routed | 48 | 21.7 | **0 / 15** |

Shale punishes a slow win: it returns its fatal hit at second 32 if still alive. An unfed Maul beats that return hit; an unfed Striker wins the first beat but not the duel. With a route, both clear. The powered stock at the relevant sampled beat is exactly 29 after a one-charge Striker hit, and 28 after a two-charge Maul hit (both started the beat from the 30-charge cap). This checks one fit and one supply contrast on a declared playable checkpoint, not the best weapon in every fight, a representative first-acquisition time, an acquisition rate across seeds, or late-wall calibration. The authored 10-minute cadence and weapon profiles are tuning values, not inferred from the historical balance matrices.
