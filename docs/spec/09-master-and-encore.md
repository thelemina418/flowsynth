# 09: Master and Encore (framework)

## Fixed

- Max radius 5.
- **The Master** is a dark structure in ring 5, visible from the start of the arc. It completes when **every oscillator type is resonating at once**, with all four crafts active, while a large ν total accumulates across eras. Its progress survives prestige.
- **Automation (granted)**: auto-prestige at a player-set Arete threshold.
- **Completing the Master** shows a quiet finale: no fanfare text, one line, `Mastered.` Then **Encore** begins.
- **Encore** is the endless post-game. Each completion of the Master multiplies the next, and numbers pass 1e308.
