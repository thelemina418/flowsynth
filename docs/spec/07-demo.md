# 07: Demo (framework)

## Fixed

- Max radius 3.
- Mixer opens when the marked Schist falls. **Effects** arrive, and each acts at a distance or across an area. Starting types: **Delay** (copies a neighbour's output to the cell 2 steps beyond it in a line), **Reverb** (a ring-1 field: ×1.2 on everything in it), **Filter** (cancels the pressure of one facing wall).
- New cables at Producer levels: **Mult** (one output to two targets at 60% each) and **Inverter** (patching into a wall deals ×5 damage).
- New Arms at Roadie levels: **Striker**, **Maul** and **Pulse**, adapted from the earlier combat kit as single-layer Arms.
- **Cross-category resonance patterns** arrive with new oscillator types, e.g. a type that resonates only when it borders an Effect and receives a cable.
- **Automation (granted)**: a console auto-buy toggle that buys the cheapest affordable fader.

## Improvise within

- The new oscillator types and their patterns.
- Further Effects. Each changes geometry, not just numbers.

## Guardrails

- One effect line per type.
- No new currency.
- Nothing produces in upgrade mode.
- No module may buy cells.

## Pace targets

- Obsidian falls at about 55–60h cumulative.
- No flat stretch over 2h.
