# 06: Console and prestige

## Console

- A strip of faders below the board, appearing the first time any fader is affordable.
  - **Channel faders**: one per type the player has.
  - **Bus faders**: one per category, from the first bus purchase onward.
  - **Master section**: after the first prestige.
- Each fader is a vertical throw showing its level, with a readout beneath: `Sine · 6 · 105`, meaning name, level, next cost. Click buys +1; shift-click buys the most affordable.
- Multipliers, bases and growth are as in `mechanics-sketch.md`.
- A type whose class isn't open yet doesn't appear, because the console shows only types the player has.

## Prestige

- `Prestige` appears as the Goal once it's available (era ν ≥ 1e6).
- Confirming shows two plain lists, **Resets** (ν, fader levels) and **Keeps** (board, cables, classes, allowance, Arete, walls conquered), plus the Arete to gain.
- The master section holds 3–5 Arete faders (the builder authors them): slower cost growth for channel faders, slower cost growth for bus faders, and a global ν multiplier.

## Acceptance

- Pace runner: the first worthwhile prestige at about 8–10h. Later Sketch eras run 4–8h.
- After prestige the board is identical and production resumes at the first tick of the next flow session.
