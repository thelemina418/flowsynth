# 08: Record (framework)

## Fixed

- Max radius 4.
- **Sync** cables arrive. Each makes two oscillators count as adjacent for one resonance pattern. They're scarce and late.
- Deeper breather oscillators: two new types beyond Release, each with a breather-only resonance pattern.
- The outer walls (Granite, Quartz, Corundum) carry harder modifiers.
- **Corundum** is the hardest wall in the game. It's visible from the start of the arc, and conquering it delivers a large burst of new content at once.
- **Automation (granted)**: Arms with nothing to fight retarget to the weakest bordering wall.
