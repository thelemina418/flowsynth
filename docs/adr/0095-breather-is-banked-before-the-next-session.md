# The breather is banked for before the next session

A completed flow session banks one breather, and at most one is held. The player takes it before the next session rather than immediately after, and it pays out only if completed. Breather-only effects are kept. This supersedes the use-it-or-lose-it offer timing in ADR-0073. Offering the break right after the session pulled players away at the moment they most wanted to spend. Banking it lets upgrade mode come first, so the breather sends the player away from the screen when they are done.
