# World unlocks through prestige and collection growth uses drafts

Accepted design; implemented by #526–#530. Final desktop/phone visual acceptance remains outstanding; implementation is not a visual-acceptance claim. See the [confirmed feedback brief](../world-layer-feedback-brief.md).

World unlocks permanently on the first prestige that banks positive Arete. This originally granted one Column alongside the once-only starter Blade; #544 expanded it to the full kit defined in the [combat redesign brief](../world-combat-redesign-brief.md). A current-era nous gate re-hides a persistent collection after reset; a Catalog entry purchase obscures the discovery behind another transaction. Enemy pressure remains before unlock but must be explained outside the hidden map.

Columns cease to be Arete purchases. The original mixed-draft rule gave a 25% chance of one World candidate after unlock, equally split between Columns and Blades, retaining at least two module choices. #545 retains this chance and module floor but routes the new World slot to Columns or Conduits; Blacksmith earns ordinary weapons through credited practice. Special drafts and previously generated offers are unchanged. Hone remains a type-wide Arete purchase. This trades the open-ended Column sink for collection-driven acquisition. Past Column spending is not refunded, by the sole player's explicit choice; existing collection is retained.

This supersedes ADR-0074's conflicting World entry/acquisition and experimental refund promises for this scope, and ADR-0079's premise that Columns remain the open-ended Arete sink. It does not rewrite frozen historical calibration or save-translation arithmetic, nor claim that those recordings validate this new economy. Existing unrelated reset and claim-grant contracts remain intact.
