# One board layer, and cells only by conquest

The World layer folds into the board. Walls occupy cells on the same single layer as modules, and conquering a wall is the only way to gain a cell. Cells are never bought. Victories stay lifetime, as in ADR-0088. We chose this so cells stay scarce and every new cell is earned through the board's own geometry, and because two stacked layers were too much to track. This supersedes the distance pricing in ADR-0089, the World-layer parts of ADR-0087, and Charge Column lifting from the cell below.
