# Progression map

The macro shape of the game. The numbers are targets for the pace runner, not formulas.

## Pacing law

Cost always grows faster than production. When growth slows, the game opens a new unlock rather than flattening the curve. Each arc adds a new kind of play and automates one the player has mastered.

## Two bottlenecks

- **Real hours buy options.** Class levels (from credited flow time on attuned habits) grant allowance and new module types. Nous cannot speed them up.
- **Nous buys strength.** Console faders raise types and categories.
- **Charge fights walls.** Conquest is fed by charge through Arms and cables, never by spending nous.
- **Arete buys speed.** The master section makes the faders climb faster after each prestige.
- **Arcs grant automation.** Automation is never bought.

## Horizon

Assumes about 1.5–2 credited hours per day.

| Arc | Credited hours (cumulative) | Max radius | Cells in reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sketch | 0–15h | 2 | 19 |
| Demo | 15–60h | 3 | 37 |
| Record | 60–140h | 4 | 61 |
| Master | 140–250h | 5 | 91 |
| Encore | 250h+ | 5 | 91 |

The opening board has 4 open cells. Every other cell starts as a wall. Rings beyond the current arc are dark.

## Arcs

| Arc | New play | Automated | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sketch | Oscillators and resonance, Modulators and Patch cables, Roadie and Arms at Flint, the console | none | First prestige (worthwhile at about 8–10h) |
| Demo | Mixer and Effects (opened by the marked Schist), Mult and Inverter cables, cross-category resonance patterns | Console auto-buy (toggle) | Conquer Obsidian |
| Record | Sync cables, deeper breather oscillators, the outer walls | Arms auto-target the weakest bordering wall | Corundum, the hardest wall in the game |
| Master | The Master project | Auto-prestige at a player-set threshold | Finish the Master, then Encore |

## Classes

| Class | Craft | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Engineer | Oscillators | At the start |
| Producer | Modulators and Cables | At the start |
| Roadie | Arms | First contact with Flint (ring 1) |
| Mixer | Effects | Conquering the marked Schist (ring 2) |

- Class level thresholds grow roughly quadratically: 0.5, 1.5, 3, 5, 8, 12, 17, 23, 30h on that class, and onward.
- Each level grants +1 allowance of the class's craft. Milestone levels grant a new type instead.

## The authored map

- There is one hand-authored map of 91 cells. Its walls fall for life, and prestige never restores them.
- The nine wall kinds sit in bands outward: Shale and Basalt (ring 1), Flint and Schist (rings 1–2), Gneiss and Obsidian (rings 2–3), Granite and Quartz (rings 3–4), Corundum (rings 4–5).
- Walls within a band can carry a **modifier**, e.g. `Only Pulse damage` or `Pressure ×2`, making placed, designed puzzles.
- HP grows super-exponentially by band.

## Prestige

| | |
|---|---|
| Resets | Nous and fader levels |
| Keeps | Cells, placement, cables, class levels, allowance, Arete, lifetime victories, real-life records |
| Arete | Earned from the era's ν on a concave curve; spent in the master section |
| Cadence | First at about 8–10h; later eras of 4–8h in Demo, lengthening in later arcs |

## Master and Encore (framework)

- The **Master** is visible as a dark structure in ring 5 from the start of the Master arc. It completes when every oscillator type is resonating on the board at once, with all four crafts active, while a large ν total accumulates across eras.
- **Encore** is the endless post-game, in which each completion of the Master multiplies the next. Numbers are expected to pass 1e308 (ADR-0094).
